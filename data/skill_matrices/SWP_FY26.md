# Swoop (SWP) skills matrix — FY26

- **Source:** FY26 Swoop Corporate Governance Statement, Recommendation 2.2 (Tier 1). Extract: `data/src/SWP_AR2026.md`.
- **Format:** qualitative list of target skills only. **No counts, ratings or per-director attribution.** Not comparable to Telstra or Megaport.
- **Categories (verbatim):** Technical skills (incl. "technology"); Experience (incl. "telecommunications, cyber security, IT/software and systems").
- **Board claim:** current members have "an appropriate mix of skills"; may consider "enhancing its technical skills in marketing and information technology integration in the medium term".
- **Comparison with scoring frame:** 1 of 4 directors at L4 (Reid); none at L5+. Board itself flags an IT gap.
