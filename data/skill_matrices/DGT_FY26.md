# DigiCo Infrastructure REIT (DGT) skills matrix — FY26

- **Source:** FY26 Corporate Governance Statement, Recommendation 2.2 (Tier 1). Extract: `data/src/DGT_AR2026.md`.
- **Format:** 12 skills, each marked "Yes" for the board as a whole. **No counts, ratings or per-director attribution.**
- **Skills listed (verbatim):** Executive leadership; ASX listed entities; Board experience and governance; Corporate strategy; Accounting and finance; Legal, compliance and regulation; Risk management; Capital management; Government and public policy; Commercial and material transactions; Sustainability; Financial services.
- **Not listed:** technology, data centres, digital infrastructure, cyber security — although the Group is "a diversified owner, operator and developer of data centre assets" and has a Technology and Security Committee.
- **Comparison with scoring frame (all 5 scored):** all five directors L1, including all three members of the Technology and Security Committee. The matrix's omission of technology matches the scored evidence.
