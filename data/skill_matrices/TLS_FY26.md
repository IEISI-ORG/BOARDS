# Telstra (TLS) skills matrix — FY26

- **Source:** Telstra 2026 Corporate Governance Statement, 13 Aug 2026, p.5 (Tier 1). Summary and extract: `data/src/TLS_CGS2026.md`.
- **Population:** non-executive directors only (8 at the matrix date: Dunn, Blok, Brenner, Chestnutt, Kramer, Lamont, Long, Loudon). CEO Brady excluded.
- **Method (company's words):** "Based on a moderated self-assessment." "A self-assessment and calibration process against these skills categories was undertaken this year."
- **Scale (3 levels):** High competency, knowledge and experience / Extensive experience and knowledge / General working experience and knowledge.
- **Format:** aggregate pie charts, no per-director attribution, no numbers printed. Counts below are read from pie-slice angles (8 NEDs → 45° per director). **Evidence grade P** until confirmed.
