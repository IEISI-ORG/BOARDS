## Summary
- **Board (8):** O'Connell (Chair), Tyler (CEO & MD), Clark, Stewart, Kelton, Livesey, Turner, Crammond (from 1 Sep 2025). All non-executives independent per the AR.
- **Committees:** Audit; Risk and Compliance; Remuneration and Nomination. No technology committee.
- **Skills matrix:** qualitative list, no counts. Lists "Network Engineering", "IT, Software & Systems" and "Cyber Security" as skills "currently represented on the Superloop Board".
- **Infrastructure:** fibre, subsea cables, fixed wireless (Jun 2025 release).
