## Facts (paraphrased)

- **Michael Venter:** to retire as CFO and to step down as an Optus Board director in 2026; to remain until March 2026. He was previously interim CEO.
- **Andy Giles Knopp:** appointed CFO from April 2026 (from Aussie Broadband).
- **CIO:** John McInerney appointed from November 2025; Mark Potter to leave in March 2026.
