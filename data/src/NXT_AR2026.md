## Summary
- **Board (8):** Flynn (Chair since 30 Apr 2014; NED since Sep 2013), Scroggie (CEO & MD since Jun 2012), Davis, Smith, Doyle, Leftakis, Page (from 1 Nov 2025), Ibrahim (from 1 Nov 2025). Clark and Lambert resigned 13 Nov 2025.
- **Independence (CGS 2.3):** "Seven of the eight Directors who served in FY26 are independent"; only Scroggie is not. Resolves the report's "NED vs Independent NED" labelling gap.
- **Committees:** Audit and Risk (ARC); Remuneration and Nomination (REMCO, chair Davis); Investment Committee (IC, chair Smith, includes Scroggie). No technology committee.
- **Skills matrix:** narrative only, no counts or ratings (see below).
