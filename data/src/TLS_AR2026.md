## Summary
- 9 directors: Chair Dunn, CEO Brady, 7 other NEDs. No technology committee.
- Elana Rubin AM retired 20 Mar 2026; Holly Kramer appointed same day.
- No mention of Bridget Loudon retiring (report claim E21 unconfirmed by this source).
- Only network-operations line experience on the board: Eelco Blok (KPN Network Operations; Dutch National Cyber Security Council co-chair 2011–17).
- Management (not board): Kim Krogh Andersen, GE Network, Product & Technology (NPT, formed 2026); Shailin Sehgal (GE Global Networks & Technology) left 30 Jun 2026; Steven Worrall CEO Telstra Digital Infrastructure (joined Sep 2025).
