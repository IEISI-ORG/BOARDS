## Facts stated by the company (paraphrased)

- **Cloud on-ramps:** 15 direct on-ramps to cloud platforms across its data centres.
- **Connectivity services listed** (three):
  - AXON, a software-defined network-as-a-service interconnection platform.
  - Data Centre Interconnects: links between customer environments in NEXTDC data centres in the same metropolitan area.
  - Intercapital connections: point-to-point Ethernet between AXON-enabled data centres Australia-wide and interstate cloud on-ramp locations.
- **Peering:** offered through dedicated ports, remote peering and cloud exchanges.
- **Subsea cables:**
  - SC1 Sunshine Coast hosts the landing station for the JGA-S cable (Japan-Guam-Australia South, about 7,000 km).
  - P2 is connected by the Superloop Indigo cable to Sydney and Singapore.
  - D1 Darwin is the landing station for the Vocus DJSC cable (Darwin-Jakarta-Singapore Cable).
  - The page states that Project Horizon will connect Perth to D1 Darwin, NE1 Newman and PH1 Port Hedland.
- **Facility list:** the page lists facilities with floor area and power capacity, including sites in development or planning (for example AK1 Auckland, TK1 Tokyo, KL1 Kuala Lumpur).

## Not stated on this page

ASNs, IP address resources, RPKI, IPv6 and MANRS are not mentioned. The routing figures are in `data/routing/NXT.md`, from APNIC RDAP, RIPEstat and the RPKI data set.
