```text
Sydney, 1 June 2026 TPG Telecom Board changes TPG Telecom Limited (ASX: TPG) (the Company) today announces the appointment of Nerida Caesar as an Independent Non-Executive Director of the Company.
The appointment follows a Board search to fill the vacancy arising from Robert Millner’s retirement on 8 May 2026.
Ms Caesar will also join the Audit and Risk Committee, the Remuneration and Governance Committee, and the Nomination Committee.
Ms Caesar brings more than 39 years’ experience in senior commercial and business leadership roles, with deep expertise in technology-led and data-driven organisations across Australia and international markets.
She was Group Managing Director and CEO, Australia & New Zealand, of Equifax (formerly Veda Group Limited), where she led the business through a period of significant transformation and growth.

Ms Caesar is currently a Non-Executive Director of Westpac, CreditorWatch, O’Connell Street Associates, Land Services SA & Land Services WA (as a nominee Director of the Public Sector Pension Investment Board of Canada) and also serves as an advisor to technology start-ups.
Ms Caesar holds a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of New South Wales, an MBA from Melbourne Business School, and is a Graduate of the Australian Institute of Company Directors.
TPG Telecom Chairman, Canning Fok, said: “Ms Caesar is a highly experienced director who has served on boards and in executive roles at some of Australia’s leading companies, including in telecommunications and financial services.
```

## Extract (trimmed to the sentences relied on): Millner retirement (2 Apr 2026)

```text
Sydney, 2 April 2026 TPG Telecom Board change TPG Telecom Limited (ASX: TPG) (the Company) today announced the retirement of Mr Robert Millner from the Board of the Company, effective immediately following the Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Company to be held on Friday, 8 May 2026.
Mr Millner, who was due to retire at the AGM, has decided not to seek re-election.
TPG Telecom’s Chairman, Canning Fok said “We thank Robert for his contribution, commitment and guidance over the past five and a half years and wish him all the best.” Mr Millner said “It has been a privilege to serve on the TPG Telecom board following the successful merger in 2020.
The business is in good shape following the expanded regional mobile network and recent fibre sale to Vocus Group and it is the right time for me to leave the board.” The Company is conducting a search for a new Non-Executive Director to fill the casual vacancy left by Mr Millner’s retirement after the AGM and will update shareholders when that process has concluded.
```
