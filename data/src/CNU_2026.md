## Summary
- Paul Reid joins the Board 1 Oct 2026; independent; People, Performance & Culture Committee; stands for election at the ASM on 4 Nov 2026.
- Jack Matthews resigns effective 28 Oct 2026 after nine years (joined 2017).
- Kate Jorgensen (joined 1 Jul 2020, independent; Chair of Audit and Risk Management Committee) stands for re-election.
- Chorus has an NZX waiver allowing its constitution to bar Associated Persons of other NZ telecommunications services from being directors.
- **Identity check:** Chorus's Paul Reid (NZ governance career: NZ Post Deputy Chair, Christchurch Airport, Foodstuffs South Island, Canterbury Scientific, NZ Growth Capital Partners; ex-Chair Volpara, ex-director Datacom) is **not** Swoop's William (Paul) Reid (management consultant, Swoop since 2008).
