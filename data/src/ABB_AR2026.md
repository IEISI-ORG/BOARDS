## Summary
- **Board (4 now; 5 from 6 Oct 2026):** Fitzpatrick (Chair, ind.), Klose (ind.), Adam-Gedge (ind.), Omeros (executive). Rousselot joins 6 Oct 2026 and **"is considered an independent Director by the Board"** (Notice of AGM).
- **Departures:** Greene retired at AGM 14 Oct 2025; Barclay resigned 23 Feb 2026 (executive chair of Green Square DC; possible re-appointment discussed in ~12 months); Britt resigned 24 Mar 2026, and his Special Technical Advisory Services Agreement "concluded by mutual agreement".
- **Board search:** "intends to appoint two new Directors" (24 Mar 2026). Rousselot is the first.
- **Committees:** Audit, Risk and Compliance; People and Community; Nominations. No technology committee. Executive directors are not committee members.
- **Director training FY26:** SOCI requirements, directors' duties, cyber security.
- **AGM:** Friday 23 Oct 2026, 10:00am (Melbourne).
- **Rousselot degree:** not stated in any ABB document. The report's "Masters in Engineering (École des Ponts), MIT Sloan MBA" is unverified.

In reaching this decision, Graeme and the Board have agreed that his resignation is the most appropriate governance outcome for the Company and its shareholders.
