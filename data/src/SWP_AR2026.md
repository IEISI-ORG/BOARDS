## Summary
- **Board (4):** Anthony (Tony) Grist, Executive Chairman (Chair from 6 Jul 2026 per website); Jonathan Pearce, Executive Director; William (Paul) Reid, NED; Janine Rolfe, NED (appointed 9 Jun 2026).
- **Departures:** Matthew Hollis and James Spenceley (NEDs) resigned 9 Dec 2025. The Chairman, CEO and CFO left in 2H FY26 (Chairman's letter).
- **Independence (CGS 2.3/2.4):** only Janine Rolfe is independent. Majority-independent recommendation not met; Chair is not independent.
- **Committees:** Audit and Risk Committee disbanded June 2026; no nomination committee. The full Board performs these roles. No technology committee.
- **Skills matrix (CGS 2.2):** qualitative list only, no counts or ratings. The Board "may however consider the enhancing its technical skills in marketing and information technology integration in the medium term."
- **Infrastructure:** Swoop "owns and operates fixed wireless" network; Melbourne Fibre Project sold ($11.0m, completion 1H FY27); Vonex stake sold Oct 2025. Still meets the inclusion test.
- **AGM:** 26 Nov 2026; director nominations close 8 Oct 2026.
